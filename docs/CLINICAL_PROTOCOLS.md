# Clinical Protocols and Product Boundaries

- **Child pathway:** Well-Child Visits span 1 month–6 years and include growth,
  development, milestones, immunization, hearing, and vision. The current
  passport summarizes records available in the app; external visit schedules
  and clinical records are not connected.
- **Adult pathway:** Intended for ages 50–75 and aligned with IFHAS. The
  implemented baseline and hearing/vision forms collect self-reported context.
  Cognitive screening is unavailable until an approved instrument, authorized
  materials, and clinical governance are configured.
- **Interpretation:** Do not treat a single result as a diagnosis. Context such
  as language, hearing, vision, sleep, and mood can affect screening. Current
  check-in trends are descriptive only; no automated meaningful-change or risk
  classification is produced.
- **Clinical review:** Clinicians enter result status (`WATCH`, `ACT`, or
  `REFER`), risk labels, notes, and next actions. The patient result page
  displays these only after a clinician records them. A referral is created
  only when the clinician selects `REFER`; the app does not infer a clinical
  referral.
- **Urgent concerns:** Nura does not assess emergencies. Users are directed to
  local emergency services or a qualified healthcare professional.
- **No fabricated data:** Never display a cognitive score, result, appointment,
  or external-data connection unless it is provided by an authorized source.
