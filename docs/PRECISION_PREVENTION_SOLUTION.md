# VitaBridge Precision Prevention — Working Solution

## Product outcome

VitaBridge connects adult brain-health prevention and child preventive-care
records in a family-centered experience. The current build delivers a safe,
data-backed first slice of the adult journey and a connected passport for
profiles already associated with the signed-in account.

## Adult journey in the app

1. **Build a baseline:** Adults can record health history, wellbeing, function,
   sleep, activity, and optional self-reported metabolic measurements.
2. **Record hearing and vision:** A separate form stores the person's reported
   sensory experiences. It is not a diagnostic test.
3. **Check in:** Adults can save sleep duration and/or a mood rating once per
   Abu Dhabi calendar day. Submitting again updates that day's record.
4. **Review the journey:** The dashboard surfaces the next available step,
   optional check-in status, six prevention domains, descriptive
   self-reported trends when enough observations exist, and clinician-entered
   review/referral information.
5. **Use Nura:** Nura explains recorded next steps, helps the user think
   through common participation barriers, and directs clinical concerns to a
   care professional. The Nura conversation and daily check-in support English
   and Arabic based on the saved adult profile preference. Nura does not
   diagnose, estimate a cognitive score, book appointments, or call an external
   AI service; broader app localization remains future work.
6. **Clinical follow-up:** Authorized clinicians can review submitted adult
   context and record their own decision, risk label, note, and next action.
   The system creates a referral only from a clinician-selected `REFER`
   decision. Users can see clinician reviews in screening history and follow-up
   in the passport.

## Connected family passport

The passport and notifications are generated from saved profiles, screening
records, and referrals rather than demo data. Every lookup is restricted to
the signed-in account. Child records appear only when already linked to that
account; the app does not yet link profiles across separate accounts.

## Safety and evidence boundaries

- No MoCA or substitute cognitive instrument is included or scored. An
  approved instrument, licensed/authorized materials, and clinical approval
  are required before cognitive screening can be enabled.
- Risk labels and referral decisions are clinician-entered, not generated by
  the app. Check-in comparisons are descriptive self-reports, not clinical
  change detection.
- Metabolic values are optional self-reports, not verified laboratory or
  device readings.
- Nura is a bounded guide, not a medical provider. It does not confirm clinic
  availability or complete bookings.
- Missing app data is not proof that care was missed.

## Remaining work before the full product vision

1. Approve and integrate a cognitive instrument, including licensing,
   administration, scoring, language versions, and clinical governance.
2. Integrate authorized clinical, laboratory, genomic, and wearable sources
   with consent, provenance, and correction workflows.
3. Connect appointment search/booking and referral status updates to provider
   systems; add reliable scheduled reminders and delivery status.
4. Implement longitudinal change detection only after clinical validation,
   population-specific thresholds, monitoring, and clinician oversight.
5. Expand Arabic localization and validate culturally appropriate content with
   local users and clinicians.
6. Add household consent/linking, production audit trails, privacy controls,
   and operational monitoring.

The current implementation is therefore a functional prevention workflow,
not a claim that the external integrations or clinical validation in the
broader vision are already complete.
